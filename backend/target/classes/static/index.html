The problem

Plant-health help is scattered and arrives too late

Farmers and home gardeners, especially in regions with limited local-language digital support, often spot leaf disease only after it has progressed.

Late detection

Disease is usually noticed only after it has progressed, when treatment options are already limited.

Fragmented sources

Plant identification, disease information, care guidance and scheme details live in separate, non-integrated places.

Language barriers

Most digital tools are not built for regional-language users, so guidance doesn't reach the people who need it.

Costly mistakes

Delayed or incorrect identification leads to unsuitable treatment decisions and avoidable crop loss.

The proposed solution

One pipeline, from photo to action

A single web platform for image-based plant identification and disease detection — a CNN model served through a Python API, integrated with a Spring Boot and MySQL backend.

01

Upload

Farmer uploads a leaf or plant photo from the browser.

Result maps to treatment steps and eligible government schemes.
